Kabadiwala Connect

Give your scrap a new beginning.

One chain, five kinds of account, and a dashboard for each. Nobody sees another role's tools.

  1. 1

    Customer

    Lists scrap, gets offers, watches the weight and the settlement.

  2. 2

    Collector

    Finds pickups nearby, collects, sells on to an area shop.

  3. 3

    Area shop

    Publishes rates, buys from collectors, sells to the mandi.

  4. 4

    Wholesale mandi

    Buys in bulk from shops, bales and sells to recyclers.

  5. 5

    Recycler

    Takes material at the end of the chain and processes it.
